Fire Pattern Predictor

Wildfires move fast. This app reads what NASA's satellites see from orbit and turns it into a picture of where fire is burning, and where it is likely to go next.

  • Live detections from MODIS, VIIRS and Landsat, merged into one consistent view.
  • Choose any zone on Earth: draw it on the map or type its coordinates.
  • Fire patterns at a glance: intensity, timing and spread across the days you choose.
