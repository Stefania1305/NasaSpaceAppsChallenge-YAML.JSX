# Fire Pattern Predictor: 30 s launch video

**Reference:** the Infinity SaaS explainer: floating app window on a soft orb ground, a hand cursor clicking a bottom dock, the
window sliding between views, then logo + CTA. Same grammar here, but on a dark ground with fire-coloured orbs.

**What it is:** a web app that takes NASA's satellite fire detections for any zone (or any country) and predicts how each fire
grows, moves, merges or burns out over the next 3 days, using an AI model and the hourly weather forecast.

**Angle:** "Where will this fire spread next?": a question, answered on screen by the real app.
**Hook (0–2.5 s):** that question building word by word over drifting embers.
**Highlights:** pick a country in one click · the AI pipeline (architecture, not detail) · the tour: spread day by day,
humidity, wind, a fire's own forecast card, the charted sidebar.
**Punchline:** the backtest number (68% of fire outcomes called right, vs 39% for "every fire keeps burning"; from
`backtest/report.md`, 1,503 fire-days), then the logo and "Open the fire map".
**Tone:** default/app-store: clean, punchy, soft transitions.
**Identity:** ground `#07090c`, fire orange `#fb923c` / `#ea580c` / `#fdba74`, IBM Plex Sans + Mono (the app's own fonts).
Watermark "AI generated video" bottom-right on every frame.
**Real UI:** all app views are real screenshots of the running app (Paraguay, data 10–14 Nov 2026, prediction 15–17 Nov). No
personal data on screen. Note: the app reads 5 days of satellite data and predicts 3 days ahead; the video says exactly that.
